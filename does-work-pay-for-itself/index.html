talk it through

The maths is only part of it. Here are a few questions to think through as you navigate this decision.

  1. Besides money, what would going back give us? Think career, adult conversation, confidence, and super.
  2. Besides time with the baby, what would staying home give us, and what would it cost us?
  3. How long is "for now"? Pick a date to look at these numbers again.
  4. If I'm home, what is my own spending money, so I never have to explain a coffee?
  5. Who carries the household's money admin and the mental load of it? Is that fair?
