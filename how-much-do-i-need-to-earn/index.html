talk it through

The maths is only part of it. A few questions to think through together.

  1. Which number of days feels right for us, before we look at the dollars? What does the maths say about that?
  2. Could we trim our monthly expenses to shrink the gap, or is there another income we haven't counted?
  3. Is the hourly rate we need realistic for the roles we'd actually apply for? Where could we check?
  4. How long would we try this for? Pick a date to look at these numbers again.
  5. Who does what on the days one of us is at work, so it doesn't all land on one person?
